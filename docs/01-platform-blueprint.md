# Kiamina Platform Blueprint

## Platform Architecture

Kiamina Platform is organised into three major layers:

1. Client Experience
2. Firm Operations
3. Financial Intelligence

## Client Experience

Clients can:

- Upload documents
- Respond to requests
- View work status
- Access unaudited financial statements
- Access monthly management reports
- View read-only accounting information
- Download reports
- Communicate securely with the engagement team

Clients cannot:

- Post journals
- Modify accounting records
- Change mappings
- Delete approved records

## Firm Operations

Internal users can manage:

- Clients
- Documents
- Reviews
- Engagements
- Tasks
- Compliance
- Payroll
- Tax
- Advisory work
- Administration
- Analytics

## Financial Intelligence

This layer powers:

- AI document processing
- Accounting recommendations
- IFRS checks
- Mapping intelligence
- Management commentary
- Strategic advisory
- Forecasting
- Risk analysis

## Core Engine

Documents feed into accounting records. Accounting records feed the General Ledger. The General Ledger feeds the Trial Balance. The Trial Balance feeds reporting, tax, payroll, advisory, and analytics.
